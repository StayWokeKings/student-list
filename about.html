Our Story

Local expertise, professional systems

Meridian Property Management has spent nearly two decades helping owners get more from their real estate — without the day-to-day burden.

Who We Are

Built by property owners, for property owners

Meridian Property Management was founded to close the gap between big, impersonal management firms and small operators who couldn't offer modern systems. We combine both: dedicated local teams with the reporting, screening, and maintenance infrastructure of a much larger company.

Today we manage residential, multi-family, and commercial properties across the region on behalf of individual investors, family offices, and HOAs — always with the same goal: protect the asset, and make ownership simple.
